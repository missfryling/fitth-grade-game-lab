🦖 Jurassic Dream Escape!

Bob the scientist is trapped in a dinosaur dream! Run, jump, collect coins, defeat dinosaurs, and escape through six prehistoric worlds.

Controls
A / D or arrow keys = Move
SPACE / W / UP = Jump
X = Energy blast
ESC = Pause

Watch out! Dinosaurs can attack as soon as they touch Bob. The final level has a giant T-Rex boss!

Help Bob escape the dinosaur dream!
