🐭 MOUSE TRAP!

Meet Billy Bob! He's a hungry little rat with a backpack. Help him collect cheese in a giant kitchen without getting caught by the cat, the owner, or mouse traps.

🧀 Every cheese starts a mini-maze. Escape the maze to add the cheese to your backpack!

❤️ You have 5 hearts. Hide in the vents when danger gets close!

Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Press E near a vent to hide. Complete 4 kitchen levels!
