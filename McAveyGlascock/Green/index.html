🛹 SPEED CITY!

Race through 75 levels of skateboarding challenges! Collect coins, hit ramps, grind rails, spray targets, and escape the police!

A / D or arrow keys: Move
W / SPACE / ↑: Jump
X: Spray paint
S / ↓: Brake

You have 5 hearts. Avoid robots, barriers, and police. Collect 💚 health pickups to restore a heart!
