🦎 GECKOMON!

Welcome to Gecko Quest! Explore colorful worlds, collect coins, discover different geckos, and battle the geckos that chase you.

🟢 Green geckos are common. 🔴 Red geckos are tougher. ✨ Golden lizards are VERY rare and worth extra coins!

Collect 85 coins to unlock the next world. There is no maximum coin total—keep collecting!

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Press SPACE to blast nearby geckos. Press X to use a power-up.
