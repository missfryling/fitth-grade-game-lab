🐉 YUMI'S ANIMAL ADVENTURES

Meet Yumi, a cute girl who can transform into a powerful dragon!

☀️ By day, help Cute Town with fun mini-games: chop strawberries, plant flowers, bake bread, and help patients.

🌙 At night, travel to the Dark Side! Collect coins, transform into animals, defeat six levels of colorful slime blobs, and face the FINAL BOSS!

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Press E near a daytime task. Press SPACE to use dragon fire at night. Press X for your special ability.
